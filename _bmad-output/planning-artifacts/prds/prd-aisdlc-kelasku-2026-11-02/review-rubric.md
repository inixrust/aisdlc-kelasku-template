# PRD Quality Review — PRD: Kelasku (prd-aisdlc-kelasku-2026-11-02)

Reviewed: `prd.md`, `addendum.md`, against `docs/intent-kelasku.md` (frozen R1–R7, non-goals). Calibration: internal tool, one institution (about 5 admins, about 400 participants/month), team of 3, 1-week MVP, chain-top (feeds `bmad-ux`, `bmad-architecture`, `bmad-create-epics-and-stories`).

## Overall verdict

The PRD is well above average for its stakes. It has a clear two-idea thesis, a tight glossary, every rule R1–R7 traced to a FR with concrete testable consequences (including the concurrency, WIB-midnight and integer-R6 traps), and honest counter-metrics. What's at risk is the class *lifecycle*. FRs that are each sound alone contradict each other at the edges. A raised quota leaves the waitlist stuck while newcomers take the freed seats (FR-3 + FR-8 vs R3). Cancellation and promotion after the class has ended rewrite an already-exported certificate list (FR-11 + FR-13 + FR-15). Editing session dates silently flips eligibility. These must be decided before epics are cut, or engineers will implement the literal text and ship the contradictions.

## Decision-readiness — adequate

Most choices are stated as decisions, not "considerations". Examples: no promotion confirmation and no undo (FR-13 "Di luar cakupan", addendum §2 with reasons), email-only status lookup with the risk explicitly accepted (FR-14 "Catatan", §9), and SHOULD items named as the first to be cut (§7.1). The open questions in §10 are actually open, and each has an owner and a blocking/non-blocking flag.

The weakness: the PRD's biggest real tensions are settled with inline `[ASSUMPTION]` tags, not surfaced as decisions with trade-offs. There is no `[NOTE FOR PM]` anywhere. Whether a quota raise promotes, whether cancellation is allowed after the class ends, and what happens to attendance on cancellation are product decisions with visible user impact. Each is resolved in one bracketed line with no "what we give up".

### Findings
- **[high]** Load-bearing lifecycle choices hidden as assumptions (§4.2 FR-3, §4.4 FR-11, §4.6 FR-15) — "Kuota yang dinaikkan tidak memicu promosi", "Tidak ada batas waktu pembatalan", and "Peserta yang dibatalkan setelah presensi dicatat tidak lagi tampil" each change who gets a seat or a certificate. None of them is flagged for PM confirmation, and none names the trade-off. *Fix:* turn each one into an explicit decision with a one-line trade-off, or add a `[NOTE FOR PM]`, and get Rina/PM confirmation before `bmad-create-epics-and-stories`.

## Substance over theater — strong

There is little furniture here. The vision (§1) is specific to this product ("Kuota dijaga sistem, bukan ketelitian koordinator"; "Rekap adalah hasil sampingan presensi"), and you could not swap it into another PRD. The personas earn their place: Rina drives the 1366×768 density and the "Penuh" marker, and Dimas drives the 360 px layout and the "Daftar tunggu #2 is not an error" stance. The NFRs have product-specific bounds (NFR-7: about 240 classes / about 5,000 registrations, < 1 s, CSV < 2 s; NFR-3: 4.5:1 contrast). There is no innovation section. That is correct for this product.

### Findings
- **[low]** NFR-7 bound lacks a measurement basis (§5 NFR-7) — "setiap halaman dan aksi selesai dalam < 1 detik di server kecil satu inti" does not say p50/p95 or server-side vs. end-to-end, and "server kecil" is undefined. *Fix:* state "p95 server response time, measured on the seeded one-year dataset" and name a reference machine (e.g. the team laptop).

## Strategic coherence — strong

The thesis (system-enforced quota; recap as a by-product of attendance) directly shapes prioritization: Epic 1 (R1–R3, R7) goes first "karena R1 adalah metrik utama" (§7.1). SM-1/2/3 map one-to-one onto the intent's three success metrics, and SM-C1/SM-C2 are real counter-metrics tied to specific FRs (FR-18, promotion). The MVP is clearly problem-solving in kind.

### Findings
- **[medium]** SM-C2 rationale is wrong and props up a questionable decision (§8 SM-C2) — "Mendorongnya (mis. promosi saat kuota naik ...) bisa mengorbankan R1" is false. Promoting up to the new quota cannot exceed the quota, and FR-13 already uses `min(kuota − terdaftar, panjang antrean)`. This faulty reasoning is the only justification for deferring quota-raise promotion (§7.3), which causes the critical finding under Done-ness. *Fix:* remove the R1 claim from SM-C2. Justify the deferral on effort only, or reverse it.
- **[medium]** SM-3 and SM-C1 are not measurable with the FRs as written (§8) — SM-3 needs "kelas yang rekap CSV-nya diekspor pada tanggal sesi terakhir", but no FR records export events. SM-C1 relies on "koreksi pasca-simpan yang sering", but no FR records attendance edits, and change history is deferred to COULD (§7.3). SM-1 "pernah punya Terdaftar > kuota" also cannot be reconstructed from current-state data. *Fix:* add a minimal FR to log CSV exports (class, timestamp) and attendance save/correct events. Otherwise redefine SM-3/SM-C1 as manual observation with a named method.
- **[low]** SM-4 has no threshold (§8) — "Target: turun nyata". *Fix:* give a number, e.g. "turun ≥ 50% dari perkiraan awal Rina".

## Done-ness clarity — thin

Taken one at a time, the MUST FRs are good. Almost every one has concrete, checkable consequences with exact UI strings ("Kuota minimal N (jumlah terdaftar saat ini)", "Bisa diisi mulai {tanggal}"), boundary values (title 1–120, name 1–100, 360 px), and concurrency tests (FR-8 "20 paralel → tepat 5", FR-9 double-click, FR-13 concurrent cancels). FR-16's 23.30/06.00 WIB cases are exemplary.

The verdict is "thin" because FRs contradict each other at class-lifecycle edges. An engineer who implements each FR exactly as written will ship the defects below. Some SHOULD FRs also have no testable consequences.

### Findings
- **[critical]** A quota raise lets newcomers jump the waitlist (§4.2 FR-3 vs §4.3 FR-8, FR-10; R3) — FR-3 says "Kuota yang dinaikkan tidak memicu promosi". FR-8 assigns "Terdaftar bila jumlah Terdaftar < kuota". Scenario: a class is full (20/20) with 5 waitlisted, and Rina raises the quota to 25 because demand is high (the most natural reason to raise it). The 5 people waiting stay "Daftar tunggu", and the next 5 people who click the link get "Terdaftar" ahead of them. That breaks the FIFO intent of R3 and the promise in UJ-2 ("naik otomatis"). FR-4 then shows a class that is not "Penuh" but still has a waitlist. *Fix:* pick one. (a) Promote `min(kuota baru − terdaftar, panjang antrean)` inside the FR-3 save transaction. This is the same mechanism as FR-13 and moves it from COULD to MUST. Or (b) amend FR-8: "Terdaftar hanya bila jumlah Terdaftar < kuota **dan daftar tunggu kosong**", and explain how waitlisted people ever get the freed seats. Add a matching R3 test.
- **[high]** Cancellation and promotion after the class has ended corrupt the certificate list (§4.4 FR-11, FR-13; §4.6 FR-15; §4.3 FR-7) — FR-11 says "Tidak ada batas waktu pembatalan". FR-15 removes a cancelled participant from the form *and* the recap grid. FR-13 promotes regardless of class state. FR-7 closes registration once the last session has passed, but nothing stops promotion into an ended class. Result: a participant who attended 4/4 cancels (or someone who knows their email cancels them, §9) after the CSV went to the certificate office. They vanish from the recap, and a waitlisted person is promoted into a finished class with "—" in every column and "Belum layak". *Fix:* decide the lifecycle explicitly. Recommended: once the last session date is < hari ini, block cancellation and promotion (API-enforced, with a message like "Kelas sudah selesai"). Also decide whether cancellation after the *first* session is allowed. Add testable consequences for both.
- **[high]** Attendance ownership on cancellation and re-registration is undefined (§3 Glosarium "Presensi", §4.3 FR-9, §4.6 FR-15) — Presensi is "catatan ... satu peserta Terdaftar pada satu sesi", but FR-9 allows a cancelled participant to re-register as a *new* Pendaftaran. Is attendance keyed to the peserta (email) or to the pendaftaran? If a participant cancels after session 2 and re-registers, do their 2 "Hadir" come back? And FR-15 "tidak lagi tampil": is the attendance kept or deleted? *Fix:* state that presensi belongs to a pendaftaran, is kept (not deleted) on cancellation but excluded from grid/CSV, and is not carried over to a new pendaftaran. Or state the opposite. Add one consequence per case.
- **[high]** Session-date edits silently change eligibility and registration state (§4.2 FR-3, §4.7 FR-19, §4.3 FR-7) — FR-19: "Status langsung berubah ... setelah tanggal sesi ditambah atau dihapus". After the CSV is exported, adding a date turns "Layak" participants into "Belum layak". Deleting an unrecorded past date shrinks the denominator. Adding a future date to an ended class reopens registration under FR-7 (registration closes based on the "sesi terakhir"). FR-3 only protects dates that "sudah punya presensi". *Fix:* add FR-3 consequences: (1) a class whose last session is < hari ini cannot get new dates (or state that it intentionally reopens); (2) the UI warns when an edit changes the number of sessions after attendance exists; (3) a past date without attendance can or cannot be deleted. Choose explicitly.
- **[medium]** FR-3 does not restate validation (§4.2 FR-3) — FR-2 requires ≥ 1 session date, no duplicate dates, and a 1–120 character title. FR-3 allows "menambah atau menghapus tanggal sesi" but does not say whether deleting the last date, or changing a date into a duplicate, is rejected. *Fix:* add "Semua validasi FR-2 berlaku saat mengubah kelas; menghapus tanggal sesi terakhir yang tersisa ditolak."
- **[medium]** Double cancellation of the *same* registration is not covered (§4.4 FR-11–FR-13) — FR-13 covers "dua pembatalan bersamaan", but the wording reads as two different registrations. A participant and an admin cancelling the same registration at the same time, or a double-click on the confirm step, must promote exactly once and must not overwrite `cancelled_by`. *Fix:* add "Membatalkan pendaftaran yang sudah Dibatalkan tidak mengubah apa pun dan tidak memicu promosi; pembatal pertama yang tercatat."
- **[medium]** Attendance state for participants added after a session was saved is unspecified (§4.6 FR-15, FR-18) — FR-18's "semua hadir" default applies only to a form "yang belum pernah disimpan". When a promoted participant joins after session 2 was saved, reopening session 2 shows them how? Pre-checked, unchecked, or "—"? And does saving write a "Tidak hadir" record that then locks the date under FR-3? *Fix:* state that participants without a record in a saved session appear as "belum dicatat" (not pre-checked), and that saving records them only if the admin touches them. Or state the alternative.
- **[medium]** FR-20 has no testable consequence and an ambiguous base (§4.7 FR-20) — "sesi yang belum tiba" leaves out *past sessions not yet recorded*, but FR-2 explicitly supports catch-up attendance. A participant could show "Tidak mungkin layak" and then flip back once Rina records a past session. There is also no integer formula, and it doesn't say whether the third status appears in the grid, the CSV, and the status page. *Fix:* define it as `(hadir + sesi tanpa catatan presensi) × 5 < total sesi × 4`, list where it shows, and add 2–3 concrete examples like FR-19's.
- **[medium]** The SHOULD BOM undermines a MUST consequence (§4.8 FR-22) — "huruf non-ASCII tetap terbaca utuh" is MUST, but Excel on Windows renders UTF-8 without a BOM incorrectly. If the SHOULD BOM is cut per §7.1, the MUST consequence fails in the stated target tool. *Fix:* make the BOM MUST (it is one line of code), or narrow the MUST to "valid UTF-8".
- **[medium]** Registration-closed rule is incomplete (§4.3 FR-7) — the class can be joined until the last session date, including after the first sessions have passed, which makes late joiners likely ineligible (acknowledged only in §9). The check is also not stated to be enforced in the API/transaction, and there is no rule for what the waitlist becomes when the class closes (entries stay "Daftar tunggu #N" on the status page forever). *Fix:* state the closing point as an explicit decision (the last session or the first session), make it API-enforced, and state that waitlist entries of an ended class are shown as "Kelas selesai" or hidden.
- **[low]** FR-12 has no Konsekuensi block (§4.4 FR-12) — it reuses FR-11 by reference only. *Fix:* add 2 bullets: an admin cancel records `pembatal = admin` and is visible in FR-5's Dibatalkan section.
- **[low]** FR-17 has no empty state or ordering (§4.6 FR-17). *Fix:* add "Bila tidak ada sesi hari ini, tampil 'Tidak ada sesi hari ini'; urut menurut judul kelas."
- **[low]** Adjectives without bounds (§4.5 FR-14 "nyaman dipakai", §4.2 FR-4 "tetap terbaca", §5 NFR-4 "bersih dan padat informasi") — FR-14 and FR-4 are partly rescued by 360 px / 1366×768. *Fix:* drop the adjectives and keep the measurable part (no horizontal scroll; all 4 columns visible without horizontal scroll).
- **[low]** CSV formula neutralization and file name not specified (§4.8 FR-22) — "dinetralkan" does not say how (prefix `'`?), and a title used in the file name can contain `/`, `:` or similar characters. *Fix:* specify the prefix rule and file-name sanitization.

## Scope honesty — adequate

§6 Non-goals restates all the intent non-goals and adds catalog and scheduling, and FR-level "Di luar cakupan" blocks do real work (FR-1, FR-13). The §11 assumptions index matches the inline tags exactly. Open-items density is 3 open questions + 9 assumptions + 0 notes. That is moderate, but for a 1-week green-light build, three of the assumptions (FR-3 quota raise, FR-11, FR-15) are load-bearing and unconfirmed. See the Decision-readiness finding.

### Findings
- **[medium]** No `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts at the real tensions (§4.2, §4.4, §4.6) — the rubric expects them at deferred decisions. The class lifecycle (open → running → ended) is the main unresolved tension, and it is never named as one. *Fix:* add one `[NOTE FOR PM]` to a short "Siklus kelas" paragraph in §4.2 that lists the decisions from the Done-ness findings.
- **[low]** FR-12 is tagged `[ASSUMPTION: dari brief.]` (§4.4 FR-12, §11) — if the brief states it, it is sourced, not assumed. *Fix:* replace it with a source reference, or keep the tag only if the brief also inferred it.

## Downstream usability — adequate

The glossary (§3) is strong and mostly respected. IDs are contiguous (FR-1…FR-22, UJ-1…UJ-4, SM-1…SM-4, SM-C1/C2). Each UJ has a named protagonist with inline context, and each feature group names the UJ it realizes. The addendum splits enforcement notes and a rule-named test list in a way architecture and story creation can extract cleanly.

### Findings
- **[medium]** "Sesi" is overloaded (§3 "Sesi"; §4.1, FR-1 "tanpa sesi", NFR-8 "memeriksa sesi"; addendum §6 "mekanisme sesi admin") — the glossary defines Sesi as a class meeting, but §4.1/FR-1/NFR-8 use it for the admin login session. Story and test authors will collide, e.g. "sesi admin" vs. "sesi kelas". *Fix:* use "sesi masuk" (or "login") for authentication everywhere, and keep "Sesi" for class meetings only.
- **[medium]** Class states are used but not defined (§3; FR-4 "Selesai", "Penuh"; FR-7 "sudah ditutup") — "Penuh", "Selesai" and "ditutup" drive behavior (FR-4 sorting, FR-7 rejection, and possibly the cancellation block proposed above) but are not in the glossary. *Fix:* add a "Status kelas" entry: Dibuka / Penuh / Ditutup / Selesai, each with its exact condition in WIB terms.

## Shape fit — strong

This is an internal tool with one external-facing actor (participants on mobile). Four UJs, each carrying a specific edge case, are the right density and not over-formalized. Personas are limited to the two roles in the intent. Implementation detail (BEGIN IMMEDIATE, partial unique index) is correctly pushed to the addendum and marked as non-binding for `bmad-architecture`. That fits a chain-top PRD that must not pre-empt the architecture decision `AGENTS.md` reserves.
